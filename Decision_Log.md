## 3. Objectives

| # | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | `f1` Total Cost (min): fixed facility cost `Y_j`, supply, transfer, last-mile delivery, plus B2B handling cost per pallet of outbound volume. **Handling comes from the Baseline facility master (2025 basis)**: GF handling is ≈1.41× Baseline (range 1.39–1.43, i.e. inflated to 2030 with non-uniform factors), so Baseline is used directly instead of deflating. **No storage-penalty term**: in the company's model it replaces capacity in the optimized scenario (GF penalty ≈ 0.136× Baseline, a different unit/concept); our MILP uses fixed cost × `Y_j` plus a hard capacity instead | ✅ |
| 3.2 | `f2` Service, **late deliveries** (changed 2026-10-09): minimize pallets/year shipped on last-mile lanes whose lead time exceeds the target, `f2 = Σ tre_jk · X_jk`, `tre_jk = 1` if `TotalLeadTimeInDays_jk > L_max(k)`. Flow-based version of MCLP coverage (Church & ReVelle 1974; review Farahani et al. 2012): counts where goods actually go, not only whether an open facility is within reach, and needs no extra binaries. Previous wording: minimize uncovered demand `Σ d_k·U_k` | ✅ |
| 3.3 | `f3` CO2 (min, tCO2e/yr) = transport + warehousing, GLEC Framework as applied by CEL for the Company (Document/CO2, decided 2026-10-10). Transport = tonnes × (road km × EF_road + sea km × 22.35) g/t-km, EF_road by leg: NDC → facility 79 (first mile, 20 t truck), facility → facility 360.83 (mid mile, 4 t truck), NDC → customer 79 (direct last mile), facility → customer 756 (indirect last mile, 3 t van). Warehousing = outbound tonnes × 5.6 kg/t (NDC, RDC, DC: storage + handling) or 1.3 kg/t (DEPO: mainly handling). No separate fixed emission per open facility: the GLEC per-tonne factor already includes building energy (avoids double counting). Tonnes: last mile uses the group's `TonPerPallet`, transfers the national mean. Coded in Multi-Objective Optimization.ipynb 2.3.3 | ✅ |
| 3.4 | **`L_max` = Company SLA: Java ≤ 1 day, outer Java ≤ 3 days** (Assumption doc §4.7), by island of the demand group (decided 2026-10-10). With the extended last-mile lanes (4.3a) this SLA gives a real trade-off: in the cost-minimal network (f1 = 259.5 B IDR/yr) 0.7% of Java and 6.0% of outer-Java pallets are late (≈ 2,050 pallets/yr, 2.8% of demand), vs a floor of ≈ 265 pallets/yr (0.4%: 22 remote groups with no on-time lane). With the Company lane set only, the SLA gave no trade-off (99.3% of lanes on time). Lead times = distance ÷ 45 / 20 km/h ÷ 24 + sea time, last leg only (stock held at the delivering facility), except DEPO lanes: + fastest inbound leg from a stocked facility, since DEPOs hold no stock (CEL lead-time note; added 2026-10-10). Sensitivity on tighter values (e.g. 0.5 day) | ✅ |
| 3.5 | Distance for `f3`: road and sea km split per lane from `LeadTime_AllFlow` (`SeaDistanceKM`); estimated cross-island lanes use the median sea share of real lanes on the same island pair; same-island lanes without data = road only | ✅ |
